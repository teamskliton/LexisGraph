# 21.4 Limitations

---

## 21.4.1 Overview

Although the LexisGraph platform successfully demonstrates the application of Artificial Intelligence, Knowledge Graphs, and GraphRAG for regulatory compliance analysis, certain limitations remain due to project scope, available resources, and evolving AI technologies. Identifying these limitations provides a realistic assessment of the current implementation and highlights areas for future improvement.

---

## 21.4.2 Regulatory Coverage

The current implementation focuses on selected regulatory documents and organizational policies.

Limitations include:

- Limited support for regulatory frameworks.
- Manual addition of new regulations.
- Restricted domain-specific legal knowledge.

Expanding regulatory coverage would increase the platform's applicability across different industries.

---

## 21.4.3 AI Limitations

As the platform relies on Large Language Models, some inherent limitations remain:

- Responses depend on the quality of retrieved context.
- Complex legal interpretations may still require expert review.
- AI-generated recommendations should not replace professional legal advice.
- Output quality may vary with document complexity.

Therefore, compliance decisions should always be validated by qualified professionals.

---

## 21.4.4 Knowledge Graph Limitations

The current knowledge graph implementation has certain constraints:

- Graph construction depends on processed documents.
- Relationship extraction accuracy depends on document quality.
- Continuous updates require periodic processing.
- Large-scale graph management may require additional optimization.

Future improvements can further enhance graph completeness and retrieval accuracy.

---

## 21.4.5 Scalability Limitations

Although designed with scalability in mind, the current prototype has some deployment limitations:

- Performance has been evaluated primarily under moderate workloads.
- Large enterprise deployments may require infrastructure optimization.
- Horizontal scaling mechanisms are not fully implemented.
- High-availability configurations require additional deployment support.

---

## 21.4.6 Security Considerations

The platform incorporates standard security mechanisms; however, advanced enterprise features such as:

- Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA).
- Single Sign-On (SSO).
- Advanced security monitoring.
- Enterprise identity integration.

can be incorporated in future releases to further strengthen security.

---

## 21.4.7 Overall Assessment

Despite these limitations, the implemented system successfully demonstrates the feasibility of using GraphRAG and Artificial Intelligence for automated regulatory compliance analysis. The identified limitations do not affect the core functionality of the platform and primarily represent opportunities for future enhancement.

---

## 21.4.8 Summary

The limitations identified in the current implementation mainly relate to regulatory coverage, AI capabilities, knowledge graph expansion, scalability, and enterprise-level security features. Addressing these areas in future versions will further improve the accuracy, robustness, and practical applicability of the LexisGraph platform while extending its adoption across diverse regulatory environments.
