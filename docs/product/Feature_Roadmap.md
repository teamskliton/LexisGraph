# Feature Roadmap

Version: 1.0

Last Updated: August 2026

---

# Purpose

This document defines the planned evolution of LexisGraph from its current Minimum Viable Product (MVP) to a fully featured enterprise legal compliance intelligence platform.

The roadmap helps prioritize development while ensuring that new features align with the product vision and information architecture.

# Long-Term Vision

LexisGraph evolves from an AI-assisted compliance checker into a comprehensive Legal Compliance Intelligence Platform capable of supporting startups, enterprises, and law firms through explainable AI, Knowledge Graphs, and continuous regulatory monitoring.

---

# Guiding Principles

Every new feature should:

- Improve compliance workflows.
- Be explainable and evidence-backed.
- Reuse existing design patterns.
- Scale for multi-tenant environments.
- Support collaboration.
- Maintain high performance.
