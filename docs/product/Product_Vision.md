# LexisGraph Product Vision

Version: 1.0

Last Updated: August 2026

---

# Product Overview

LexisGraph is an AI-powered legal compliance intelligence platform that helps organizations evaluate internal policies against applicable laws and regulations. By combining Large Language Models (LLMs), Knowledge Graphs, and GraphRAG, LexisGraph transforms static legal documents into connected, searchable knowledge, enabling faster compliance analysis, gap detection, and evidence-backed recommendations.

Rather than acting as a traditional PDF comparison tool, LexisGraph serves as a collaborative compliance workspace where legal and compliance teams can manage documents, analyze regulatory obligations, review AI-generated findings, and maintain continuous compliance across multiple projects.

---

# Vision Statement

To become the intelligent compliance operating system for organizations and law firms by transforming fragmented legal documents into connected knowledge and delivering explainable, AI-assisted compliance decisions.

---

# Mission Statement

Enable organizations to simplify legal compliance through AI by providing accurate analysis, transparent recommendations, and a centralized workspace for managing regulations, policies, and compliance reports.

---

# Problem Statement

Organizations often struggle to keep internal policies aligned with evolving legal regulations. Compliance reviews are typically performed manually, requiring legal professionals to compare lengthy policy documents with complex legislation.

This process is:

- Time-consuming
- Expensive
- Difficult to scale
- Prone to human error
- Challenging to maintain as regulations change

Most existing solutions focus on document storage or keyword search rather than understanding relationships between legal concepts and organizational policies.

---

# Our Solution

LexisGraph combines AI with Knowledge Graph technology to create a structured representation of legal documents and organizational policies.

The platform:

- Extracts legal clauses from uploaded documents
- Builds a Knowledge Graph of relationships
- Uses GraphRAG for contextual retrieval
- Detects compliance gaps
- Generates explainable AI recommendations
- Produces interactive compliance reports
- Supports continuous compliance monitoring
