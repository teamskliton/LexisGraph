# Chapter 5 — Administrator Module

---

# 5.1 Introduction

The Administrator Module is responsible for maintaining the centralized Legal Knowledge Base (LKB) used by the LexisGraph AI engine.

Unlike organization users, administrators do **not** perform compliance analysis. Their responsibility is to ensure that the legal knowledge base remains accurate, up-to-date, and trusted.

This module forms the foundation of the entire platform because every compliance report generated by the AI depends on the quality and freshness of the legal knowledge base.

---

# 5.2 Objectives

The Administrator Module shall enable administrators to:

- Manage legal data sources
- Upload legal documents
- Synchronize government websites
- Monitor ingestion jobs
- Maintain MongoDB
- Maintain Neo4j Knowledge Graph
- Monitor AI processing
- View system health
- Manage scheduler operations
- Review processing logs

- Audit Logging

- File Validation

---

# 5.24 Acceptance Criteria

The Administrator Module shall be considered complete when:

✓ Administrators can authenticate securely.

✓ Trusted legal sources can be added, edited, disabled, and removed.

✓ Legal PDFs can be uploaded and processed.

✓ Automatic synchronization functions correctly.

✓ MongoDB updates successfully.

✓ Neo4j Knowledge Graph updates successfully.

✓ Scheduler operates reliably.

✓ Processing jobs are trackable.

✓ System health is visible.

✓ Logs are searchable.

✓ Administrative actions are audited.

---

# 5.25 Summary

The Administrator Module serves as the operational control center of LexisGraph.

It is responsible for maintaining the integrity, freshness, and quality of the Legal Knowledge Base while providing administrators with complete visibility into ingestion pipelines, graph construction, processing jobs, and overall platform health.

No compliance analysis occurs within this module; instead, it ensures that the AI engine always operates on an accurate and up-to-date repository of legal knowledge.
