The testing process confirmed that:

- Policy documents were uploaded and processed successfully.

These results demonstrate the overall reliability and robustness of the system.

Based on the testing results, the LexisGraph platform successfully met its design objectives and system requirements. The successful completion of all testing activities indicates that the LexisGraph platform is functionally correct, secure, stable, and ready for deployment.
