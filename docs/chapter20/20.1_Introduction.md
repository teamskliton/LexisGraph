# 20.1 Introduction

---

## 20.1.1 Purpose

Future Scope identifies the potential enhancements and expansion opportunities for the LexisGraph platform beyond its current implementation. Although the developed system successfully automates regulatory compliance analysis using GraphRAG, continuous advancements in Artificial Intelligence, Natural Language Processing, and Legal Technology provide opportunities to further improve the platform's capabilities, scalability, and practical adoption.

This chapter outlines possible future developments that can enhance the system's performance, usability, and applicability across different industries and regulatory domains.

---

## 20.1.2 Need for Future Enhancements

Regulatory frameworks are continuously evolving, requiring organizations to adapt their compliance processes accordingly. Similarly, advances in AI models, knowledge graph technologies, and retrieval techniques present new possibilities for improving automated compliance analysis.

Future enhancements will enable LexisGraph to:

- Support a broader range of regulations.
- Improve AI reasoning capabilities.
- Increase system scalability.
- Enhance report accuracy.
- Simplify user interaction.
- Enable enterprise-level deployment.

## 20.1.3 Objectives

The future development of LexisGraph aims to achieve the following objectives:
