# 18.9 Report Generation

---

## 18.9.1 Purpose

The Report Generation module converts the results of the GraphRAG analysis into structured compliance reports that can be reviewed by organizations. These reports summarize compliance findings, highlight regulatory gaps, provide supporting legal references, assess risks, and recommend corrective actions in a clear and understandable format.

---

## 18.9.2 Objectives

The Report Generation module shall:

- Generate structured compliance reports.
- Summarize GraphRAG analysis results.
- Highlight compliance gaps.
- Include supporting legal references.
- Provide risk assessments.
- Generate AI-based recommendations.
- Store reports for future access.

---

## 18.9.3 Report Generation Workflow

The report generation process is illustrated below.

```text
GraphRAG Analysis

↓

Compliance Findings

↓

Risk Assessment

↓

Recommendation Generation

↓

Report Formatting

↓

Store Report

↓

Display to User
```

Each generated report is based on the latest analysis results and supporting evidence.

---

## 18.9.4 Report Contents

A compliance report includes the following sections:

- Executive Summary
- Compliance Score
- Identified Compliance Gaps
- Risk Assessment
- Supporting Legal References
- AI Recommendations
- Overall Conclusion

This structure enables users to quickly understand the organization's compliance status.

---

## 18.9.5 Risk Assessment

Each identified compliance gap is assigned an appropriate risk level.

Supported risk categories include:

- Low Risk
- Medium Risk
- High Risk
- Critical Risk

Risk classification helps organizations prioritize corrective actions.

---

## 18.9.6 Recommendations

Based on the GraphRAG analysis, the system generates actionable recommendations.

Recommendations may include:

- Missing policy updates.
- Regulatory requirements to address.
- Suggested policy modifications.
- Best practices for improving compliance.

Each recommendation is linked to the relevant compliance finding whenever applicable.

---

## 18.9.7 Report Storage

Generated reports are securely stored for future access.

Stored information includes:

- Report ID
- Organization
- Generated Date
- Compliance Score
- Findings
- Recommendations
- Report Status

Reports can be viewed or regenerated when required.

---

## 18.9.8 Benefits

The Report Generation module provides:

- Automated compliance reporting.
- Explainable AI-generated results.
- Consistent report structure.
- Faster compliance reviews.
- Improved decision-making.
- Historical record of compliance assessments.

---

## 18.9.9 Summary

The Report Generation module transforms GraphRAG analysis into comprehensive compliance reports by combining identified gaps, risk assessments, legal references, and AI-generated recommendations. These reports provide organizations with clear, evidence-based insights that support regulatory compliance and informed decision-making.
