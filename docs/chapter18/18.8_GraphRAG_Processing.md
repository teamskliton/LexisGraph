# 18.8 GraphRAG Processing

---

## 18.8.1 Purpose

The GraphRAG Processing module is the core intelligence component of LexisGraph. It combines knowledge graphs, vector similarity search, and Large Language Models (LLMs) to perform context-aware regulatory compliance analysis. By retrieving both semantic and structural information from processed documents, GraphRAG enables accurate identification of compliance gaps and generation of explainable recommendations.

---

## 18.8.2 Objectives

The GraphRAG Processing module shall:

- Retrieve relevant regulatory information.
- Perform semantic similarity search.
- Utilize knowledge graph relationships.
- Provide contextual information to the LLM.
- Identify compliance gaps.
- Generate explainable compliance recommendations.

---

## 18.8.3 Processing Workflow

The GraphRAG workflow is illustrated below.

```text
User Query

↓

Vector Search (Qdrant)

↓

Knowledge Graph Retrieval (Neo4j)

↓

Context Aggregation

↓

Gemini 2.5 Flash

↓

Compliance Analysis

↓

Generated Report
```

The combined retrieval process improves the quality and accuracy of AI-generated responses.

---

## 18.8.4 Vector Retrieval

The first stage performs semantic retrieval using vector embeddings.

Responsibilities include:

- Searching similar document chunks.
- Ranking results by similarity.
- Retrieving relevant policy sections.
- Providing contextual evidence.

Vector retrieval enables efficient discovery of semantically related information.

---

## 18.8.5 Knowledge Graph Retrieval

The knowledge graph provides structured relationships between legal entities.

The retrieval process identifies:

- Regulatory clauses.
- Related entities.
- Legal relationships.
- Connected policy sections.
- Cross-referenced regulations.

This structured information complements semantic search results.

---

## 18.8.6 Context Generation

The retrieved vector results and graph data are combined into a unified context.

The generated context includes:

- Relevant regulatory clauses.
- Organizational policy sections.
- Related legal entities.
- Supporting references.
- Retrieved evidence.

This contextual information is supplied to the Large Language Model for analysis.

---

## 18.8.7 AI-Powered Compliance Analysis

The generated context is processed by **Gemini 2.5 Flash**.

The AI model performs:

- Policy interpretation.
- Regulatory comparison.
- Compliance gap detection.
- Risk assessment.
- Recommendation generation.

The generated output is supported by retrieved evidence from the GraphRAG pipeline.

---

## 18.8.8 Advantages of GraphRAG

Compared to traditional Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG), GraphRAG provides:

- Improved contextual understanding.
- Better reasoning through graph relationships.
- Reduced hallucinations.
- Explainable responses.
- More accurate compliance analysis.

These advantages are particularly valuable when analyzing complex legal and regulatory documents.

---

## 18.8.9 Output

The GraphRAG module produces:

- Compliance findings.
- Missing regulatory requirements.
- Risk classifications.
- Supporting legal references.
- AI-generated recommendations.

These outputs are forwarded to the report generation module.

---

## 18.8.10 Summary

The GraphRAG Processing module combines vector retrieval, knowledge graph reasoning, and Large Language Models to deliver accurate and explainable regulatory compliance analysis. By integrating semantic search with structured legal relationships, LexisGraph provides more reliable compliance insights, identifies regulatory gaps with greater precision, and generates evidence-backed recommendations for organizations.
